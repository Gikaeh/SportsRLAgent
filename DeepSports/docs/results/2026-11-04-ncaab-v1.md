# DeepSports college basketball (NCAAB) v1: locked-holdout result

**Question.** Can the NBA v1 approach (Elo + schedule + form, small linear models), adapted for men's
D1, predict college games? College markets are thought to be less efficient than the NBA's: does the
model get closer to the closing line, or beat it?

**Answer.** It's a real model but it doesn't beat the market. On the locked holdout (2025-26 games from
Feb 1 on, 1,949 games with full closing lines, scored once), college v1 beats a tuned Elo on log loss
(−0.008, 95% CI −0.016 to −0.001) and margin MAE (−0.65 points, CI −0.86 to −0.46). It is well
calibrated (ECE 0.024). It is still behind the closing line on every target: log loss +0.013 (CI +0.007 to
+0.020), margin MAE +0.14 (CI +0.06 to +0.24), total MAE +0.28 (CI +0.15 to +0.42). Flat bets at −110
lose: 50.7% against the spread, 49.5% on totals. **It's closer to the close than NBA v1.1 is**
(relative to the line's own error, about half the gap on margins and a third on totals), which fits
"less efficient", but it's not close enough to bet. By the declared rule it **ships as context**, not
as a betting edge.

## Holdout protocol (written to docs/DECISIONS.md before any fitting)
- **Holdout:** every completed 2025-26 (season 2026) game tipping off on or after 2026-02-01 00:00
  US/Eastern with both teams D1 (`teams.listed`): the rest of the regular season, conference
  tournaments and the postseason (NCAA tournament, NIT and others; season type 3). 1,955 games, 1,949
  with a closing moneyline, spread and total, over 60 game days (Feb 1 – Apr 6).
- **Validation (all tuning):** weekly walk-forward folds testing 2025-12-01 → 2026-01-31 (2,458 D1
  games). November (from Nov 3) is training and warm-up only. `ncaab.validate` removes every row at or
  after the holdout start before any walk-forward (tested). 2026-27 games aren't used.
- **Holdout predictions are walk-forward too:** each holdout week's models train on every D1 game
  that tipped off before that week (including earlier holdout weeks, as production would), with
  the frozen settings.
- **Frozen config:** `models/ncaab_v1/config.json` (sha256 `4f98d431…38bda124`). The holdout step wrote
  the marker `models/ncaab_v1/config.holdout-scored` and refuses to run again; the per-game predictions
  are saved and re-rendered with the `report` step.
- **Ship rule (declared up front):** ship as context if college v1 beats Elo on log loss *and* margin MAE
  with 95% day-clustered CIs excluding 0, and its ECE ≤ 0.05. Claim a betting edge only if it also beats
  the close (CI) on some target *and* a declared flat-bet lower 95% bound is above 52.4%.
- **Declared breakdowns (no others were run):** closing |spread| ≤ 5 / 5–12 / > 12; conference /
  non-conference / postseason; neutral site vs home court. **Declared extra:** a model + closing-line
  blend with weights fit on validation.

## Method
**Data.** Warehouse copy taken 2026-11-04 15:10 PT (after the 14:00 sync; the live file was last written
14:33). Season 2026: 6,300 completed college games (6,195 regular season, 105 postseason). 5,770 are
D1-vs-D1, 732 have one non-D1 side.

**Non-D1 opponents.** Every non-D1 school (`listed = false`) is pooled into one "non-D1" team for Elo
(starting at 1200, fixed) and for the ratings: each plays only 1–3 D1 games, too few to rate alone.
Their games count toward the D1 team's history (Elo, ratings, form, rest), but they are never
trained on or scored.

**No prior season.** The warehouse starts with 2025-26, so everyone starts level (Elo 1500, rating 0).
Early-season regression to the mean comes from the ridge penalty, the form shrinkage (3 prior games) and
the `min_games` filter below. Travel is dropped (unknown for most college cities).

**Features (all pre-tip; a game counts as known 4 hours after its tip-off).**
- Elo (FiveThirtyEight style, margin-of-victory multiplier), tuned for college: K 60, home edge 60 Elo points.
- **Ridge ratings** (new, `RidgeRatings`): a Massey-style regression of each team's points on its
  offense, the opponent's defense and a home edge, refit before every tip-off on the games finished
  so far, with a ridge prior of λ = 1 game against an average team. Gives `rt_margin` and `rt_total`.
  With ~360 teams and lopsided schedules, raw recent form mostly measures who you played; this corrects for it.
- The v1 schedule and form features: rest difference, back-to-backs, games in the last 7 days, shrunk
  last-10 net points and net efficiency; for totals, the league-average total, form total, pace and
  efficiency sums.
- Player features (v1.1's availability and top-N set, from prior box scores) were a candidate.
- Closing lines are never features. Historical injury lists aren't used (they leak). ESPN college summaries
  have no injury list anyway.

**Models.** The same small linear models as NBA v1 (`ModelV1`: logistic win probability, ridge margin and total,
standardized, C = 1 / α = 10, not tuned), with college feature lists. Elo's margin baseline is a straight
line fit of margin on (Elo gap + home edge) on each fold's training games.

**Validation choices** (`docs/results` tables below, all on Dec–Jan):
- Elo: K ∈ {20, 30, 40, 60, 80, 100, 140} × home edge ∈ {30, 60, 90, 120} by raw Elo log loss. Round 1
  was K 20–60 × home 60–120; its best (K 60, home 60) sat on the grid's edge, so round 2 widened it. The
  optimum stayed at K 60 / home 60, now inside the grid. (A high K suits a season with no prior: ratings must move fast.)
- Ridge λ ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8} by raw rating margin MAE (round 1 was 1–8; λ = 1 was on the edge and stayed best).
- Model, per target: team-only vs team + player features × training on all rows vs only rows where both
  teams had ≥ 5 games. A ≥ 10 filter was tried and is infeasible: no team has 10 games before
  mid-December, so the first validation folds had nothing to train on. Chosen: win = team-only, ≥ 5;
  margin and total = team + players, ≥ 5. The player features changed validation MAE by
  0.0004 (margin) and 0.02 (total): effectively a tie, chosen by the declared rule.
- Model + close blend weights (fit on validation): win 0.00, margin 0.00, total 0.07. On validation the
  best blend gave the model no weight at all for win probability and margin.

only 18 non-conference games; the conference/non-conference split can't be judged.

### Flat-bet check at −110 (backtest, not a betting record)
Bet the model's side whenever it disagrees with the closing number by at least *t* points. No pushes:
every closing spread and total in the holdout is a half-point.

| market | edge ≥ t | bets | hit rate | Wilson 95% | day-clustered 95% | ROI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| spread | 0 | 1,955 | 50.7% | 48.5–53.0% | 48.6–52.7% | −3.1% |
| spread | 3 | 394 | 50.0% | 45.1–54.9% | 44.6–55.7% | −4.5% |
| total | 0 | 1,955 | 49.5% | 47.2–51.7% | 47.3–51.6% | −5.6% |
| total | 4 | 378 | 49.2% | 44.2–54.2% | 43.6–53.7% | −6.1% |

Break-even is 52.4%. Every line is at or below a coin flip; none is close to break-even.

### Ship rule, applied mechanically
- Beats Elo on log loss (CI −0.016 to −0.001) and margin MAE (CI −0.86 to −0.46), ECE 0.024 ≤ 0.05:
  **ship as context: yes.**
- Betting edge: it doesn't beat the close on any target, and no flat-bet bound is above 52.4%: **no.**

## The hypothesis: is the college market less efficient?
Partly supported, in a narrow sense. The same kind of public-data model gets closer to the college
close than NBA v1.1 gets to the NBA close (on different seasons, so this is a comparison, not a test):

| | model − close, log loss | margin MAE gap / close MAE | total MAE gap / close MAE |
|---|---|---|---|
| NBA v1.1, 2025-26 holdout | +0.024 | 0.37 / 10.99 = 3.4% | 0.88 / 14.51 = 6.1% |
| College v1, Feb–Apr 2026 holdout | +0.013 | 0.14 / 8.78 = 1.6% | 0.28 / 13.13 = 2.1% |

But "closer" isn't "beatable": the model + close blend learned on validation gave the model zero weight on
win probability and margin. On these features the model holds no information the close doesn't
already have. The flat bets lose. If the college market has soft spots, they're in information this
model doesn't have (lineups, injuries, which ESPN doesn't publish for college), or in openers and
lower-tier games where lines are posted early. The closing line here is DraftKings / ESPN BET at close.

## Caveats
- **One season, one window.** The holdout is Feb–Apr of one season (60 game days), mostly conference
  games. Ratings are most reliable then, since teams have 20+ games. Early-season performance (Nov–Dec)
  is not covered by the holdout. The Dec–Jan validation gap to the close was about twice as large
  (+0.026 log loss, +0.60 margin).
- **Validation choices, two rounds.** The Elo and λ grids were widened once (both rounds on validation only).
  The chosen values are interior optima, and the holdout was never looked at in between.
- **Elo's pooled non-D1 start (1200)** and the form shrinkage were fixed, not tuned. They matter mostly in November.
- **Conference flags** come from the raw ESPN summaries (`conferenceCompetition`), not the warehouse. All
  6,300 season games have one.
- **What the holdout touched:** only the single scoring run. Its feature columns and model class were fixed
  before it, from NBA v1 and from validation.

## Recommendation
**Ship as context, not as a betting signal**, the same footing as NBA v1.1. It's calibrated, clearly
better than Elo, and only 0.14 points of margin behind the close. That makes a reasonable "Prediction:"
line for Nevada and the rest of the D1 slate. Show it with the closing line next to it, not as picks.

**What wiring would take** (not done here, per the brief):
1. A college path in `predict_upcoming` / `pretip_predictions` (`league="ncaab"`, model version
   e.g. `ncaab-v1`): build rows with `college_rows`-style features for the current season, add
   `rt_margin`/`rt_total` for **scheduled** games (today `ridge_features` only covers completed
   ones: give it the scheduled games too, rated with all finished games), and predict only D1-vs-D1 games.
2. **A cross-season decision (untested):** 2026-27 is the first season with a prior one. Options: train on
   2025-26 rows plus the current season, and carry Elo over (×0.75 like the NBA) and/or start the
   ridge prior at last season's regressed ratings. None of this can be validated until a few weeks of
   2026-27 are in. Until then, the safest rule is to show college predictions only once both teams have
   ≥ 5 games this season (from about late November). That matches the training filter.
3. `deepsports predict --league ncaab --model-version ncaab-v1`, plus one line in `tools/scheduled-sync.sh`.
   Grading already works per league (`prediction_results` and `prediction_record(league=...)`), and the API
   and apps already show college games. The prediction card needs the college rows.
4. A small `deepsports check`-style guard: the college sanity limits (|spread| ≤ 60, totals 100–220) are
   in `market.SANE_LIMITS`. `store.PREDICTION_RESULTS_SQL` still applies the NBA total filter
   (150–320), so it would NULL most college closing totals when grading. That needs a data-engineer fix
   before college market comparisons show up in the record.

**Next steps:** (a) data-engineer: a `conference_game` column on `games` and a league-aware total filter
in `PREDICTION_RESULTS_SQL`; (b) backfill earlier college seasons (2023-24, 2024-25): that allows a
real preseason prior, multi-season validation and an early-season test; (c) log college opening lines
with timestamps. If there's a soft spot, it's more likely in openers than at the close.
