- A Circa bet is placed only when Circa's line beats WH's. In most of them WH's 21:00 line was already 0.5-1.5
  points worse for us (e.g. Nov 17: UNLV/KU u158 vs WH 156.5, ILL/DUKE u164.5 vs 163.5, FIU/UAB u166.5 vs 165.5),
  and the close then went past WH's number. Circa's "better" number looks like an off-market line the market
  corrected, not extra value.
- 4-14 or worse at a fair coin is p ≈ 0.015, but 18 bets over 4 nights is small and nights are correlated; the
  CLV gap is the more meaningful signal.
